Independence · RICS Red Book

TRevPAR Premium: 35% Conservative, KPMG-Adjacent Sources Excluded

The base TRevPAR premium of 35% is anchored to STR Global and HVS independent benchmark data. KPMG’s 65% figure — produced through Polynesia Consulting in collaboration with Henry Terou — is excluded from the headline IRR engine to avoid circular validation: the same advisor cannot both originate and verify a feasibility input. RICS Red Book independence rules require this firewall. The KPMG figure is preserved as a sensitivity reference, not a base assumption.
